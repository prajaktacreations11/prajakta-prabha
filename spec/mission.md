# Mission

प्राजक्तप्रभा exists to give प्राजक्तप्रभा's creative work — poetry, personal essays, and traditional Marathi verse — a home online that is beautiful, dignified, and entirely hers to maintain.

## Why this project exists

This is not a general-purpose blogging platform or a commercial content business. It is a **personal creative archive for one author**, built around three forms she writes in:

- **काव्य-संग्रह (Poetry)** — original poems
- **आठवणींचा ठेवा (Articles)** — memoir-style personal essays and reflections
- **उखाणे (Ukhane)** — traditional Marathi wedding verses

The site's reason for being is to make that body of work reachable, readable, and shareable — for family, friends, and anyone who wants to read Marathi creative writing presented with real care for the script and the language, not as an afterthought bolted onto an English-first template.

## Who it's for

- **The author** is the primary user of the *authoring* side of this project. She is not a developer. Every decision about how content gets added must be judged against one question: can she do this herself, without touching code, git, or a terminal?
- **Readers** are the primary user of the *public* side — people who want to read her poetry and articles, share a piece they liked, and come back for new work.

## What success looks like

- She can sit down, paste a poem she just finished, pick "Poetry," and see it live — without anyone else's help.
- A reader can find a poem, read it comfortably in Devanagari, and share it in a way that actually represents that poem (not a generic homepage link) when they send it over WhatsApp or Instagram.
- The site costs nothing to keep running and requires no server to babysit.
- The Marathi script is treated as a first-class typographic citizen — line height, font pairing, and spacing chosen for Devanagari readability, not defaulted from an English template.

## Explicit non-goals

- **Not multi-author.** There is no concept of user accounts, roles, or a second writer in the current mission. If that changes, it's a deliberate future decision, not something to build ahead of.
- **Not a monetized publication.** No ads, no paywalls, no analytics-driven growth loops are part of this mission.
- **Not a general CMS product.** The content model (poetry / articles / ukhane) is shaped specifically around what she writes — it should stay opinionated to that, not grow into a flexible everything-blog engine.
- **Not server-dependent.** The mission is best served by a site that can be hosted for free as static files. Any backend/database complexity that doesn't serve the authoring or reading experience directly is scope creep against this mission.

This document, along with [spec.md](./spec.md) and [tech-stack.md](./tech-stack.md), and the root `CLAUDE.md`, should anchor future work on this project. When a proposed change doesn't trace back to something in this mission, that's worth pausing on before building it.
