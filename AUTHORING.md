# Publishing a post — a guide for the author

This is for publishing poetry, articles, or ukhane to the site. No coding, no
technical knowledge needed — just a web browser.

## The one-time setup

Someone (whoever manages the site) needs to do two things for you first:

1. Create a GitHub account for you, using your dedicated Gmail address.
2. Send you an email invite to the project. You'll need to click **Accept invitation**
   in that email, while logged into the GitHub account from step 1.

Once that's done, you're set up permanently — everything below is what you'll do
each time you want to publish something.

## Publishing a post

1. Go to **https://prajaktacreations11.github.io/prajakta-prabha/admin/**
2. Click **Login with GitHub**.
   - First time only: GitHub will ask you to sign in (use the account from setup)
     and then ask you to approve access — click **Authorize**.
3. You'll see three sections on the left: **Poetry**, **Articles**, and **Ukhane**.
   Click the one that matches what you're publishing.
4. Click **New Poem** (or **New Article** / **New Ukhana**).
5. Fill in the fields:
   - **Title** — the title of the piece
   - **Date** — defaults to today; change it if needed
   - **Excerpt** — a short one or two line teaser (optional, but readers see this
     before they click in)
   - **Thumbnail** — a picture to go with the post (optional). Click it to upload
     an image from your phone or computer.
   - **Tags** — a few keywords, one per line (optional)
   - **Content** — the poem or article itself. This box supports basic formatting
     (bold, italic, line breaks) — the toolbar above it shows what's available.
6. When you're happy with it, click **Publish** at the top right (it may say
   **Publish now** in a dropdown).

## What happens next

Clicking Publish saves your post directly to the website's project. The live site
then takes **a few minutes** to rebuild and show the new post — this is normal,
just refresh the page after a bit if you don't see it right away.

## Editing or fixing something after publishing

Go back to **/admin/**, click the section (Poetry/Articles/Ukhane), click the post
in the list, make your changes, and click **Publish** again the same way.

## If something looks wrong

If login fails, or Publish doesn't seem to work, don't worry about fixing it
yourself — just note what you were doing and what message (if any) appeared on
screen, and pass that along to whoever manages the site.
